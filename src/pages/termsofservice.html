MailPigeon connects to one or more mailboxes you authorize and a messaging channel of your choosing. Depending on your configured delivery mode, MailPigeon sends you either a once-a-day digest of all triaged messages (ranked, most important first) or, in quiet mode, stays silent until a "requires action" email arrives and then sends a ranked digest of all messages since the last successful delivery.
